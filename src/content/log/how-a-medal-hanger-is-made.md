Every hanger starts as a design file: the name, the font and the graphic a
customer picked. From there:

1. **Cut.** The design is cut from 0.1" steel on the machines at Community
   Werkshop here in San Diego.
2. **Sand.** Edges and surfaces are sanded smooth.
3. **Powder coat.** A powder-coat finish gives the glossy color and rounds
   over any sharp edges.
4. **Pack and ship.** The family helps with packaging and labels, and orders go
   out by USPS Priority Mail.
